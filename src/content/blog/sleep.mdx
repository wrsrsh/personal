If you have any aura like me, you have tried to fix your sleep.

And you know the internet makes it look like a NASA launch sequence.

Magnesium. Mouth tape. A $900 ring. Twelve supplements harvested from the moon. Brother, just go to bed.

The sleep industrial complex is NOT going to like this, but the [Pareto Principle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pareto_principle) has already solved it.

Here is the 80/20:

1. **Keep the phone out of the bed.** AT ALL. Watch your slop before bed if you must, I am not your mother. But the bed is for sleep. Make it feel sacred. Make it feel like you are about to enter the nicest loading screen of your life.

2. **Want to sleep well.** Romanticize it. Waking up fresh is elite. Once you get a good night of sleep, your brain remembers the sauce and starts asking for it again. Positive feedback loop. WAGMI.

3. **Turn on night mode.** One or two hours before bed, make every screen less like a flashbang. Better yet, put the screens down for a bit. Revolutionary technology: looking at a wall.

4. **Do not eat a medieval feast right before bed.** Give your food two or three hours to settle. Your body does not need to run a digestion speedrun while you are trying to respawn.

5. **Sleep in roughly the same window.** You do not need to become a 5 AM warrior with a podcast microphone. Just stop giving your body a different timezone every night.

6. **See sunlight and walk after you wake up.** Go outside in the first hour or so. Let the giant fireball in the sky know you are alive. Your body clock will figure out the rest.

Hence proved, sleep is not a biohacking side quest.

Sleep more. That is the whole stack.
