I think the productivity and health gurus overcomplicate sleep too much.

Like all things, the [Pareto Principle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pareto_principle) and the bell curve of wisdom are very applicable here. We gotta do the 80/20 of this.

In the modern day, that looks something like this:

1. **Keep smart devices out of bed.** AT ALL. This is a hard and fast rule. Nothing supersedes this. Watch whatever you wanna watch before bed. Make sleep your sanctuary and be excited for it, and make it relaxing.

2. **Aspire to have good sleep.** Romanticize having good sleep and waking up fresh in the morning. The brain does a lot of things when you do this, I don't know how this works, but if you once wake up well rested and happy, the next day you will want to too. So, it becomes sort of a self fulfilling prophecy. A good positive feedback loop.

3. **Use night light / reading mode.** Use it on all devices 1-2 hours before bed. Even better, try to avoid screens 1-2 hours before bed altogether.

4. **Stop eating 2-3 hours before bed.** Generally try to have food 2-3 hours before bed and then nothing after that point until the next morning. Don't make the body digest stuff while you are asleep. A bloated stomach will RUIN your sleep.

5. **Sleep within the same 60-90 minute window every day.** Try to optimize your sleeping time instead of forcing yourself to wake up at a certain time.

6. **See sunlight and walk soon after waking up.** Try to see sunlight and walk outside in the first 60-90 minutes after waking up. This optimizes your body's internal clock to start waking up around that window automatically.

And the best advice: just sleep more.
